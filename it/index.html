Welcome!

~ Restaurant ~

Location

Staro Zdanje Restaurant is located at one of the most beautiful and historically rich spots in Novi Sad, at 1 Trg Marije Trandafil. Situated in the heart of the city, in an authentic and quieter part of the old town, the restaurant offers a perfect escape from the city crowds while remaining close to the main sights.

A unique experience

A visit to this restaurant is a special experience thanks to its unique atmosphere, reminiscent of a cosy, romantic cellar with a warm, homely feel and a touch of nostalgia. Relaxed, comfortable and peaceful, it is ideal for couples, family gatherings or meeting friends. The charm of the place also comes from the blend of tradition and the dedication of young hospitality professionals, whose enthusiasm and professionalism ensure attentive service and warm hospitality.


Cuisine

The restaurant's culinary concept draws on a rich tradition and carefully selected recipes. The menu offers a variety of choices inspired by the best of local and international cuisine and tailored to today's food lovers. From delicious starters and hearty main courses to excellent desserts and coffee, the kitchen caters to guests of all generations and offers a delightful culinary experience.

Quality food

Quality ingredients and careful preparation are what make Staro Zdanje stand out. Guests regularly praise the fresh, flavourful food and its thoughtful presentation. Alongside outstanding dishes, the restaurant offers an excellent selection of drinks, including quality wines and refreshing beverages, making every visit a satisfying experience.
